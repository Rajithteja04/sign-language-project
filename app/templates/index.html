Live Demo Guidelines

  1. Frame yourself: Stand about 1.5 m from the camera so face, torso, and both hands stay visible.
  2. Neutral pause: Hold a relaxed pose for ~1 s before each sign; the system clears its buffer during this pause.
  3. Perform the sign: Execute one LSA64 sign smoothly over ~1 s, keeping motion centred and continuous.
  4. Hold for recognition: Wait ~2 s after finishing so confidence ≥ 0.82, margin ≥ 0.20, and the 7-frame vote confirm the word.
  5. Watch the chips: Start the next sign only after the new chip appears; otherwise gestures may blend together.
  6. Recover if needed: If you see “Low confidence” or “Ambiguous sign,” re-centre your hands and repeat the sign.

Tip: Use “Clear Sentence” to reset the sequence, and keep lighting even so MediaPipe never loses your hands.
